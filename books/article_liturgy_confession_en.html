﻿A Prayer Of Repentance

With A Contrite Heart

Alex M. Kuruvilla

Important Note
We cannot obligate our holy and sovereign God to act just by repeating a prayer like a formula. Prayer is not a mantra. For a prayer to be meaningful, you must understand what you are saying and let your heart agree with the words you speak. If your heart does not match your words, the prayer becomes empty and hypocritical. God hates hypocrisy (കപടഭക്തി). Such prayer does not bring blessing—instead, it may bring God’s displeasure. Therefore, be careful when you pray. Do not rush. First, read this prayer slowly. Understand each word. Let your heart truly desire what the prayer says. Only then come before God with sincerity and humility.

Loving Heavenly Father,

I come to You in the name of Your Son, Jesus Christ. I come to receive Your mercy and the grace I need. Please have mercy on me, O God.

Abba Father, I confess that I have gone my own way, like a lost sheep. I chose my own desires instead of Your will. I have sinned against You and disobeyed Your commandments. I have done what is wrong in Your sight. I acknowledge that I am a sinner by nature and by my actions. I have failed to love You and to love others as I should. I am truly sorry for my sins, and I turn away from them.

Abba Father, I believe that Jesus Christ is Your only Son. I believe that You sent Him to save me from my sins. I believe that Jesus took my guilt upon Himself and died on the cross in my place. I believe that He was buried and that You raised Him from the dead on the third day.

Abba Father, I ask that the blood Jesus shed on the cross be counted as the ransom paid for my life. Save me from the judgment that is to come. Cover me with the righteousness of Your Son. Because of Jesus’ sacrifice, please forgive my sins and be at peace with me. Let Your face shine upon me.

Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me. Do not cast me away from Your presence. Restore to me the joy of Your salvation and make me willing to obey You.

I offer this prayer in the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Savior. Amen.
