FAQ

  1. How do I read an eBook bought from Amazon?
    Install Amazon Kindle app on your phone or laptop/Desktop PC. Log in using your Amazon username and password. Download the purchased eBook to your phone from within the app. You can also read other eBooks you purchase with that app. Alternatively, if you own a Kindle Reader, you may download the purchased book into the Reader.
  2. How do I read free eBooks on my phone?
    The EPUB files – if any are posted on this site – may be read using any EPUB Reader app; Recommended Android App: Moon+ Reader or Amazon Kindle App.
  3. How do I read eBooks on my computer?
    On a Desktop computer or a Mac, install the free Calibre eBook Management program and import the book(s) into the program.
  4. Can I share these resources with others?
    You are permitted to share any free eBooks posted on this site — provided you do not alter the files.
  5. How can I support the author?
    You are welcome to buy Amazon Kindle eBooks published by the author.
